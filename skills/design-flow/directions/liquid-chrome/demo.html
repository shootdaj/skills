    Is the Orbit 2 launch on track for November?

    Pre-orders opened a week ago and 12,480 people have reserved one, which is 73 percent of the first batch and more than twice the first Orbit's opening week. The product page is live in four regions and early reviewers rate it 4.8 out of 5. Two things need attention: the Mint finish is running low, with 420 left, and the Chrome edition sold out in three hours, so people who missed it need a clear next step.
