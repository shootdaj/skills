    Report opening

    Is the Lisbon footage safe to wipe from the cards?

    Six memory cards came back from the trip holding 1,284 clips. Before a card is wiped, every clip on it should exist in two other places: the portable drive and the cloud backup.

    We checked each clip against both copies. 1,102 clips have both, 179 have only the drive copy, and 3 have no copy yet. Those 3 are on card E.

    • Cards A to D are safe to wipe today.
    • Card E holds the 3 clips with no copy; copy it first.
    • 179 clips wait for the cloud, about 6 hours on home wifi.
    • Next step: wipe A to D, then run this check again tomorrow.
