    Motion7.1

    Bobble on hover

    Blocks wiggle when the pointer lands on them, or when you tap. Cards and numbers on this page do it too.

    MotionForm7.2

    Bubble cluster that settles

    Headline numbers float up and settle into a cluster; size follows the number. Tap one to give it a nudge.

    MotionForm7.3

    Bottom dock navigation, with magnification

    The real dock sits at the bottom of the screen and takes you to each section. On a desktop, icons grow under the pointer. This copy shows it in place.

    MotionForm7.4

    Sticky-note takeaways you can drag

    Throw a note and it glides, then settles inside the board. With a keyboard, focus a note and use the arrow keys.

    Form7.5

    Isometric city for the architecture

    Each building is one part of the footage system; little dots ride the roads between them. Hover or focus a building to lift it.

    3D7.7 · optional

    Instanced toy blocks

    One draw call for every brick (three.js instancing). Drag to spin. Without WebGL it falls back to a flat drawing.

    Form7.6

    Kanban for the plan

    Drag cards between columns. With a keyboard, focus a card and use left and right to move it, up and down to reorder.
