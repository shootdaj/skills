      Three short, realistic pieces in this look: a report opening, a dashboard strip and a side-by-side choice.

      Report opening

      Is the coast trip footage ready to edit?

      Over nine days we shot 82 hours of video on four cameras. Every card was copied to two drives and checked byte by byte before it was wiped, and 1,284 clips are now searchable by place, camera and time of day. Two things still stand in the way of a smooth edit: 41 clips need a quick look because the tagger was unsure, and 3 drone clips have no proxy, the small copy that plays smoothly in the editor.

      • 2 verified copies of every clip, so the cards can be reused.
      • 1,284 clips found by place, camera or time of day.
      • 41 clips to review before the rough cut, about 20 minutes.
      • 3 proxies to rebuild, about ten minutes on the laptop.
