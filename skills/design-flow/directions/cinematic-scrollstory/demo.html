      In use

      Three short, realistic pieces in this look: a report opening, a dashboard strip and a choice between two cuts.

      Report opening

      Is the launch film ready to publish on Friday?

      The film for the product launch is a 12-minute cut with the picture locked, meaning no more edits to the shots themselves. A test group of 40 people watched it this week and 71 percent stayed to the end, well above the 50 percent we aimed for. Two things are left before Friday: colour on four scenes, which is routine, and two music cues without a licence, which could force a swap if the label does not answer by Wednesday.

      • Picture locked at 12:04, after two rounds of notes.
      • 71% watched to the end in a test group of 40.
      • 4 scenes still need colour, about one day of work.
      • 2 music cues unlicensed; backups are ready if needed.
