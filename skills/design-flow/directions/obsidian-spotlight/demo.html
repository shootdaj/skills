    07 · In use

    A report, a dashboard, a comparison

    Three short pages in this look, with sample content about a footage library.

    Report opening

    Should the footage library move to the new drive this month?

    The library lives on one portable drive that is 94% full. We copied the whole library to the new 8 TB drive and then compared every file, byte for byte.

    All 2,140 clips matched (measured). The copy took 3 hours 10 minutes, and search on the new drive answered in the same time as before (measured on 40 searches).

    Your call: switch the library to the new drive, or keep both drives in step for one more trip.
    How we checked
    1. Copied every clip to the new drive.
    2. Compared each file's fingerprint on both drives.
    3. Ran the same 40 searches on both.

    Not tested: the drive's speed when it is nearly full.

    • Nothing lost in the copy: 2,140 of 2,140 clips are identical.
    • Search just as fast: 210 ms before, 212 ms after.
    • Room to grow: the new drive is 31% full with everything on it.
    • One cable to swap: the new drive needs the USB-C cable, not the old one.
